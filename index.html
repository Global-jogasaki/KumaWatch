Benchmark Result Summary (Yamagata, primary setting)
① GLM-Logit (best-ranked): Recall@20 = 0.547 · margin over static prior B1 not significant (+0.014, p = 0.155)
② HierBayes: Recall@20 = 0.542 — no improvement over GLM-Logit (p = 0.624) · confidence-filtered subset top-50% days: 0.639 (exploratory only; does not validate cell-level uncertainty)
③ TTM (IBM Granite) + Extra Trees: significantly worse than GLM-Logit (p < 0.001) · Jaccard@20 GLM vs TTM = 0.55 — moderate disagreement; not evidence of useful complementarity
