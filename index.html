Three-Layer Architecture
① Primary: GLM-Logit — L2-regularized logistic regression (cell fixed-effects + rolling30 + log-annual + seasonal harmonics + year_idx)
② Uncertainty: HierBayes — hierarchical Bayesian Poisson (PyMC + NumPyro) → graduated alert strategy · Recall@20 0.542 → 0.639 (top-50% conf.)
③ Complementary: IBM Granite TTM (zero-shot) + Extra Trees · Jaccard@20 GLM vs TTM = 0.55

Data: Yamagata & Akita prefectural wildlife observation databases (public) · Code: Apache 2.0 · Data: CC BY 4.0
